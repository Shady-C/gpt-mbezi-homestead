# Surface brief: house-viewer.html

Scope: the whole viewer page (3D cutaway, floor plan, site plan, connections, room board, room list, lightbox, downloads), plus index.html and the icons. Mode: Operate (the visitor finds a room, reads its size, checks its photos).

Audience and job: family and co-owners opening a WhatsApp link on a phone; find any room, see its estimated size and photos, understand where it sits and which doors lead on. Constraints: PRODUCT.md, the spec's Part 2, sizes always labelled estimates, no north arrow, no location data, no third-party requests.

## Direction contract

Direction pinned by the owner in chat (hand-painted duka signboards, chosen over kanga cloth, airport wayfinding and a standard clean app); no concept-seed roll ran.

THESIS: Every room of the house is a hand-painted duka signboard: a flat field of enamel colour, a painted border and keyline, name and size lettered in a sturdy signwriter sans. It refuses the category default of a grey app shell with a tinted sidebar and one accent.

OWN-WORLD: Lime-washed wall ground (#E7E9E4), signwriter black ink. Painted fields by kind of space: yellow day rooms, cobalt bedrooms, green washrooms, white board for corridor and porch, black board with yellow letters for outside. One action colour, signwriter red, for the chosen tab, the chosen room and the way on. Square boards, 3px ink border, 2px white keyline inset; Lilita One lettering for names and sizes, Atkinson Hyperlegible for running text. Pictograms drawn in code, flat.

STORY: The visitor sees the house in 3D with a small painted sign on each room (name and size), taps one, and a board rises: the size as the largest figure, the confidence and its basis in one sentence, the photos as numbered cells, and "Doors from here" with arrows to the next rooms. They walk the house board to board and believe the sizes as honest estimates.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone 390 x 844: a yellow signboard header (house pictogram, "House Mbezi", the estimate note) about 110px tall; four painted tabs (3D, Plan, Site, Connections) 48px tall; the 3D cutaway in an ink-bordered scene filling the rest of the screen above a strip of view tools. Desktop 1440: the same header and tabs over the scene, the room board as a 420px side panel on the right.

FORM: Signboard world over a standard viewer shell (header, tabs, canvas, side panel or bottom sheet, list). Signature move: the doors-from-here walk, the 3D view easing to the room entered. Raises kept: doors listed as the choices ahead (airport wayfinding); photos as numbered cells (event catalogue); one fixed scale per drawing with a painted scale bar (botanical folio); size as the dominant figure on each board (type specimen). Honest risk: it can get loud; colour stays on rooms, signs and the one action, the ground stays quiet, one painted field leads per screen. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
