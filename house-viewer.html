How this was drawn

Layout follows the owner’s sketch: full-width kitchen at the top, porch and gate on the left, corridor on the right, Bedroom 2 beside parking. Single storey, no stairs.

The 68 photos establish what each room holds. Sizes, opening positions and wall heights are estimates from the photos and the sketch, each with its confidence. No roof geometry or north direction has been established, and none is shown.
